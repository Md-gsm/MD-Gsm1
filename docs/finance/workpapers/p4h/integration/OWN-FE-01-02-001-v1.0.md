## 2. FE-01 — goods sale

1. Revenue recognition is based on fulfilled delivery and transfer of control, supported by the committed Inventory fulfillment/movement evidence; invoice finalization or receipt alone is insufficient.
2. Sales owns the commercial document, immutable line snapshots and commercial handover confirmation/reference. Inventory exclusively owns committed stock movements, fulfillment records, stock status and valuation. Sales may reference Inventory evidence but must not create or mutate Inventory movements.
3. Sales document finalization, physical/commercial handover, Inventory commitment, revenue recognition, payment receipt and settlement are distinct facts and states.
4. Partial and multi-stage fulfillment are supported through explicit line-to-fulfillment relationships. Corrections, returns and reversals are represented by traceable new documents/events; finalized records are not silently edited or deleted.
5. Physical return, revenue/tax reversal, refund and inventory restock are separate decisions/effects. A returned item remains unavailable for sale until Inventory inspection and an explicit disposition (SELLABLE, DEFECTIVE, QUARANTINED or UNUSABLE).
6. Revenue reversal follows the approved return/contractual entitlement decision; inventory restock and any related inventory/COGS effect follow Inventory inspection and disposition. Neither is inferred from the other.
7. Finalized price, discount, quantity, UOM, currency, terms and applicable tax are immutable snapshots. Document-level discount allocation uses eligible base proportionally and deterministic remainder allocation by stable line identity.
8. Monetary arithmetic uses the approved currency base unit and integer-safe deterministic rounding. The detailed shared Money policy must specify positive/negative ties, remainder distribution and proportional refund behavior before runtime implementation; no arbitrary tolerance or account mapping is inferred.
9. Payment, allocation, settlement and revenue recognition are separate. PSP receipts remain in the appropriate funds-held-at-PSP state until actual settlement. Credit terms are snapshotted at document finalization; default partner terms are 30 days and configurable.
10. Exact tax treatment, account IDs, posting dimensions and effective rates are resolved only from approved Tax/Pricing sources and canonical COA/Posting Matrix evidence.

## 3. FE-02 — repair service

1. Repair lifecycle facts (case, service line, execution, technical completion, required quality control, readiness, handover and operational closure) are distinct and traceable. A service line is the unit for independently measurable obligations and any partial recognition.
2. Technical completion is recorded by an authorized technician using server-derived actor and time. Quality control is a separate event only where the approved workflow requires it, and must be performed independently where policy requires.
3. For a standard physical repair, revenue recognition requires technical completion and confirmed handover to the customer or an authorized recipient. Technical completion, invoice finalization or payment alone is insufficient.
4. An alternative customer acceptance without physical handover is allowed only for a separately identified service-catalog item whose approved policy explicitly permits it and whose independent evidence is retained. It is not a general substitute for handover.
5. Repair intake/acceptance remains disabled in R1 and must display: «پذیرش تعمیر در نسخهٔ بعدی فعال می‌شود.» This prohibition must not be bypassed by introducing a hidden or partial RepairCase intake workflow.
6. R1 may record actual repair-related financial facts only when they originate from a real, authorized, independently addressable source that exists in R1 (for example, an already-supported external source with immutable references). If no such source exists, FE-02 financial posting remains unavailable in R1. No synthetic RepairCase, fabricated lifecycle event or manual status substitution is permitted.
7. Full repair workflow, WIP and repair profitability reporting remain R2 scope. WIP is disabled in R1.
8. Inventory alone owns committed part-consumption movements and valuation. RepairOperations stores references only. Parts/COGS, outsourced service expense/liability, service revenue and technician commission are distinct accounting concerns.
9. Outsourced-service principal/agent treatment requires explicit evidence and an approved rule; account 2114 may be used only where canonical COA and supporting evidence establish eligibility.
10. Technician commission entitlement is separate from service revenue. R1 may recognize only a supported entitlement/accrual if the required source and rule exist; full payroll calculations remain R2. Payout is not authorized by this record and requires a separately approved payment/settlement flow.

## 4. Shared event, identity and delivery controls

1. Financially relevant source facts carry stable EventId, EventType, ContractVersion, AggregateId, AggregateVersion, SourceDocumentReference/version, OccurredAtUtc, RecordedAtUtc, server-derived ActorReference where applicable, CorrelationId and CausationId.
2. ActorReference identifies the authenticated operator/system actor. The customer or representative receiving goods/device is a distinct RecipientReference with authorization/evidence; it must not be conflated with ActorReference.
3. Party identity is referenced by canonical PartyId. Avoid copying PII or payment credentials into cross-module events.
4. Monetary fields (Currency, SourceAmount, tax snapshot references) are required only for event types that actually carry monetary facts; per-event schemas must define required/optional/nullability. Non-monetary events must not be forced to invent amounts or currency.
5. EventId is the immutable event identity. Request IdempotencyKey is a separately scoped stable request key; the two are not interchangeable.
6. Idempotency is end-to-end across source persistence, outbox, consumers, Inventory references and Accounting posting. Same identity/key with a different canonical payload fails closed. Retries reuse the original identity and must not duplicate effects.
7. Source document, event and audit record plus Outbox record are persisted atomically in the owning database transaction. Publication occurs only after commit; delivery is at-least-once with idempotent consumers. No exactly-once transport claim is made.
8. Corrections and reversals are new traceable facts linked to prior source identity/version. Historical finalized facts are immutable.
9. Contract versioning and compatibility are explicit; each event schema defines its own required fields, nullability, precision and source lineage.

## 5. Accounting controls

1. RecognitionTime is an Accounting decision and must be explicitly determined from approved source evidence; it is never inferred automatically from OccurredAtUtc, RecordedAtUtc, invoice time, payment time or status labels.
2. Payment receipt, allocation, settlement, revenue recognition, tax liability, inventory movement, COGS, refund and commission entitlement are separate events/rules.
3. No Posting occurs without an accepted rule, eligible canonical account mapping, approved amount/tax basis, open period, idempotency, actor/source evidence, atomic transaction and audit trace.
4. No account code or account attribute is inferred from account prefix/title. COA v1.2 remains master authority and v1.4 its current supplementary register; unresolved mappings remain blocked.
5. R1 WIP is disabled. R2-only scope cannot be activated by a source event or posting workaround.
6. Accepted positive Posting Rules remain 0 until each rule is separately evidenced, implemented, tested and reviewed.

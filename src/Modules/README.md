# Modular application boundary

This directory records the approved target topology. Business modules are introduced only after their own G1 authorization. Cross-cutting security foundations require a separately bounded, owner-approved implementation scope and their own technical/security gates; they do not authorize consuming business modules. Each module owns its Domain, Application, Infrastructure and versioned Contracts. Consumers reference published Contracts only.

Current state: six Contracts assemblies are compiled and included in MDGsm.sln. IdentityAccess has partial server-side runtime. Finance has a narrowly scoped runtime slice for Party subledger account identity and merge participation, referencing only published Finance and Persons Contracts. Persons has partial BuildingBlocks runtime for Party registration, temporary workflow retention and controlled merge coordination. AuditSecurity now has a provider-neutral Domain/Application core and versioned Contracts on the O20/O21 closure branch; its PostgreSQL adapter, production schema, source Outbox integrations, worker, platform signer, independent immutable anchor, verifier deployment and operations remain absent. The AuditSecurity core is not a production service and does not authorize DDL, grants, credentials or activation.

These slices do not constitute complete business modules or G1 acceptance. Production migrations and G1 authorization remain prohibited until their independent gates pass.
